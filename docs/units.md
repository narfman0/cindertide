# Cindertide — Units

---

## Overview

All human factions share a base roster. Each faction has exclusive elite units that define their playstyle. The full roster is not available at mission start — units unlock progressively through campaign progression and tech tier advancement.

Robot units are available early via the Foundry but become unavailable during the Act 2 Ironborn conflict. They return after the peace is brokered, now with Ironborn-flavored variants.

---

## Availability Progression

Units unlock across the campaign in three waves:

- **Wave 1 (Act 1, Tier 1–2):** Core infantry, basic vehicles, basic support
- **Wave 2 (Act 1–2, Tier 2–3):** Heavy weapons, specialized infantry, light armor
- **Wave 3 (Act 2–3, Tier 3):** Heavy armor, robots, air support, faction elites

## Faction Elite Units

Industrial efficiency, heaviest armor, early robot access.

| Unit | Tier | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| Unit | Tier | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vanguard Squad | 2 | Elite infantry, high morale | Expensive, never breaks, reduced aura dependence |
| Inquisitor | 2 | Hero-adjacent support unit | Single entity, disrupts ancient units specifically, weak to conventional fire |

## Robot Units (Ironborn)
Available via Foundry. **Unavailable during Act 2 Ironborn conflict.** Return after peace with upgraded Ironborn variants.

| Unit | Tier | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laborer Frame | 2 | Combat engineer replacement | Faster repair, self-repairing over time, no fortification building |
| Chassis Fighter | 2 | Core robot infantry | Higher HP than infantry, no morale state, expensive |
| Ironborn Walker | 3 | Heavy robot combatant | Equivalent to heavy tank in role, faster, no crew to suppress |
| Carrier Unit | 3 | Robot transport + repair | Carries one unit squad, repairs adjacent robots passively |

**Post-peace Ironborn variants** gain visual Ironborn markings and a small stat bonus reflecting their willing cooperation vs earlier forced deployment.

## Ancient/Hollow Units (Enemy Only)
Players never field these. Documented for mission design reference.

### The Hollow

| Unit | Role | Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Void Crawler | Basic swarm unit | Ignores cover, suppressed by fire, overwhelming in numbers |
| Hollow Knight | Elite infantry equivalent | High HP, immune to morale effects, targets heroes preferentially |
| Dread Engine | Heavy unit equivalent | Radiates suppression aura, slow, devastating melee |
| Subsumed Human | Converted human unit | Looks human, behaves wrong — used for ambush and infiltration missions |

### The United Voice (Act 3 only)
Combines all Hollow unit types with amplified stats. New unit types introduced in final missions only — not fully documented here, revealed to player as surprise escalation.

## Unit Roster Growth Per Mission

Each mission brief shows which units are available for that mission. The pool grows as:
- Tech tier advances between missions
- Campaign events unlock specific units (Ironborn peace unlocks robot roster)
- Certain choices reward unique unit access (rescued a Covenant Inquisitor — they join your pool)

This means early missions feel lean and scrappy. Late Act 3 missions feel like commanding a full war machine.
